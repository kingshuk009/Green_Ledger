# GreenLedger presentation sequence

Use the dashboard in this order during a minor-project demo:

1. **Farm polygon / identity** — establish the registered land unit.
2. **NDVI heatmap** — show spatial greenery evidence from the real polygon-clipped raster.
3. **NDVI time series** — show repeated observations instead of a single snapshot.
4. **Actual vs predicted SOC** — show held-out model validation with MAE/RMSE/R².
5. **Baseline vs current SOC** — show the carbon-stock change that feeds the estimate.
6. **Carbon Asset card** — show estimated tCO₂e, uncertainty, model version, spatial evidence and status.

### What to say

> GreenLedger does not create a carbon number from NDVI alone. NDVI provides vegetation evidence. Soil carbon is estimated using observed soil information, spatial context and a trained SOC model. The estimate is then compared with a baseline and converted to an estimated CO₂-equivalent outcome, with uncertainty and methodology status shown separately.

### What not to say

Do not say that NDVI itself measures tonnes of CO₂.
Do not say that a Carbon Score of 80 means 80 tonnes of CO₂.
Do not call an estimate an issued carbon credit.
Do not present illustrative example values as measured results.
