What makes this asset explainable?

GreenLedger does not rely on one number. The dashboard shows the satellite-derived greenery evidence, the historical observation trend, SOC model validation, baseline-to-current SOC change, spatial-neighbor evidence, uncertainty, and methodology/status information.

A carbon asset estimate is not automatically an official carbon credit. Verification and issuance remain external to this software.
